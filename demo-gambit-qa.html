Gabu's Gambit

A pocket poker roguelike. Build a hand, collect strange little advantages, and make the numbers behave badly.

Select cards with taps or 1–9 and 0. Space plays, D discards, P pauses. R sorts ranks, S sorts suits, I opens Run Info and the 75-Joker collection. Arrows focus actions; Enter activates. This is a mock host, with live Lumiverse checks still pending.
